# Example Remediation KPI Report

> Synthetic portfolio example. No employer or client data.

**Reporting date:** 2026-09-10

## Executive observations

The sample portfolio contains five findings. One finding has validated remediation evidence. One high-severity finding is formally risk accepted and therefore remains visible as an exception rather than being counted as remediation. A critical item recorded as remediated but lacking validation evidence remains in operational backlog, demonstrating the project's evidence-based closure rule.

## Decision questions

1. Which critical/high overdue findings require immediate owner escalation?
2. Are remediation teams closing risk or primarily reducing low-severity volume?
3. Are exceptions time-bounded and supported by compensating controls?
4. Is closure evidence sufficient to demonstrate the vulnerable condition is absent?
5. Is backlog/SLA trend improving across accountable owners?

## Recommended actions

Prioritize overdue critical exposure, obtain validation evidence for nominally remediated findings, review accepted risk before expiry, and use owner-level metrics to drive accountable remediation. Re-run the same governed metric definitions after remediation to demonstrate measurable risk reduction.
